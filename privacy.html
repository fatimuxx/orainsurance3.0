Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

ORA Insurance, LLC ("ORA Insurance," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information, including in connection with our SMS text messaging program.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you request a quote, become a customer, or interact with us:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Mailing address and property/vehicle information relevant to coverage
  • Communication preferences and opt-in status for SMS messaging
  • Policy and claims information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide insurance quotes and process applications
  • Service your policy, including renewals and payment reminders
  • Communicate about claims and appointments
  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Send SMS text messages, if you have opted in

3. Text Messaging (SMS) Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third party.

Your mobile number and opt-in status are used solely to deliver the SMS communications described in our Terms of Service. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.

4. How We Share Information

We may share your information with:

  • Insurance carriers, for the purpose of obtaining quotes and binding or servicing coverage
  • Service providers who support our operations (e.g., CRM, scheduling, and messaging platforms) under confidentiality obligations
  • As required by law or regulatory request

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share SMS opt-in data or mobile numbers with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Your Choices

  • Opt out of SMS: Reply STOP to any text message, or contact us directly.
  • Access or correct your information: Contact us using the information below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
